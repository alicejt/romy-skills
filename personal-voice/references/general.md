# General writing

Establish what this piece should do and for whom before choosing a structure.
Lead with the point, scene, or tension when setup adds nothing. Keep setup when
it carries character, context, or a real admission.

Use the writer's own evidence: names, dates, numbers, decisions, examples, and
limits. Do not turn an observation into a universal lesson unless the source can
support it. End on the clearest useful point rather than recapping or adding a
fake-profound kicker.

For edits, preserve the original order unless it hides the point or makes the
piece hard to follow.
