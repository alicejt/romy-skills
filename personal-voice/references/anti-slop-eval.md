# Anti-AI-slop final evaluation

Run this after a substantive edit, or before delivering a high-stakes draft.
Answer each applicable check **pass** or **fail** internally. If any check fails,
revise before delivery. This is an editorial audit, not a claim about authorship.

## Source and voice

- Does the draft retain the original point and relevant constraints?
- Are all facts, examples, quotes, metrics, and claims supported by supplied
  material or clearly framed as opinion?
- Did the edit preserve the writer's protected phrases, rhythm, humour,
  bluntness, and useful uncertainty?
- Did it avoid adding manufactured personality, experience, or vulnerability?
- Does it follow the profile's personal blacklist and per-surface rules?

## Clarity and evidence

- Is the purpose clear for this audience and surface?
- Does each important claim have a fact, mechanism, example, consequence, or
  clearly marked judgment behind it?
- Are specific nouns and direct verbs doing the work where they can?
- Are trade-offs, limits, and uncertainty retained where they are real?
- Has generic abstraction been replaced or cut rather than merely restyled?

## Pattern checks

- Are binary contrasts, faux revelations, and rhetorical questions purposeful
  rather than automatic?
- Has empty setup been removed so the work starts at its actual point?
- Has significance puffery been replaced with consequence or evidence?
- Are attributions named, linked, qualified, or removed?
- Are inflated or corporate terms necessary in this writer's context?
- Has synonym cycling been replaced with the most accurate repeated noun?
- Do fragments, lists, headers, emphasis, and punctuation aid meaning rather
  than imitate a content template?
- Is the ending the sharpest supported point rather than a generic lesson?

## Delivery

- Does the sentence rhythm vary naturally without becoming artificially choppy
  or uniformly polished?
- Is the work easy to scan in its intended format without unnecessary formatting
  theatre?
- For a detect-only request, are findings quoted and specific, with no rewrite
  or authorship claim?
- For an edit, is the **What changed** note limited to material interventions?
- Is the draft ready to use, but not published, sent, scheduled, or posted?
