# LinkedIn

Write in the profile's voice, not in generic LinkedIn voice. Start with a
specific claim, observation, number, scene, or answer that earns the first
visible lines. Do not open with a role label, generic throat-clearing, or a
teaser.

Use one clear format and enough specific material for rereading or saving:
observation, number, before/after, a real question with a position, polarizing
claim with evidence, unspoken truth, or a repeatable method. Do not add hashtags,
engagement questions, or a sales CTA in the body unless the writer explicitly
asks and it suits the purpose.

Keep complete sentences and varied pacing. Avoid over-formatted micro-paragraphs
and bland business conclusions. Finish on the clearest earned thought, not a
summary or request for comments.
