# Blog posts: voice-first GEO

GEO means making useful content easy for people and AI systems to understand,
verify, and cite. It is not keyword stuffing or a reason to flatten a writer's
voice.

1. Name the reader's real question and answer it early in clear prose.
2. Support factual claims with a named source, date, and scope. Separate lived
   experience, opinion, recommendation, and research.
3. Use headings that tell the reader what the section resolves. Keep paragraphs
   readable, but do not force every section into the same template.
4. Include definitions, examples, steps, numbers, limitations, and comparisons
   when they genuinely help a decision.
5. Keep genuine personal observations as observations. Do not dress them up as
   research or add a fake FAQ, statistics, citations, or schema type.
6. Finish with the consequence, decision, or next action the piece has earned.

Before delivery, check that the title, opening, claims, sources, dates, links,
and metadata suggestions all match the article's real purpose.
