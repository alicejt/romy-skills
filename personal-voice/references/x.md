# X posts

Choose a format that fits the idea: a short observation, connected post, long
post, thread, reply, quote-post, image-led post, or joke. Do not cut useful
context merely to satisfy an old character limit, and do not make a thread
because the topic feels important.

Start with the claim, scene, phrase, or joke that earns attention. Avoid generic
hooks, engagement questions, manufactured controversy, and empty teasers. A
post may be funny, personal, awkward, or incomplete without becoming a lesson.

Use real source material. For a factual current event, verify it before posting.
If a link is useful, make the post independently worthwhile first and explain
why the destination is the natural next step.
