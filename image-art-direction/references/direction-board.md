# Direction board template

Present three directions before asking the user to choose one. Keep each entry
to the same level of detail so the choice is real.

## Direction name

- **Visual premise:**
- **What appears in frame:**
- **Composition and space for copy:**
- **Light, palette, and material:**
- **Why it fits the business and requested surface:**
- **Risk or tradeoff:**
- **Calibration prompt:**

Use a direction name tied to the image logic. Examples: `weathered materials at
working scale`, `editorial portraiture with compressed space`, or `bright
diagrammatic product still life`. Do not use empty labels such as `minimal`,
`premium`, `modern`, or `clean` on their own.
