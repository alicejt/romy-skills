## Check before drafting

- Does every email have a distinct reason to arrive?
- Does the timing fit the reader's actual situation?
- Does each claim have an approved source?
- Is the CTA proportional to the reader's relationship with the sender?
- Is a missing link, compliance statement, or personalization field visible?
